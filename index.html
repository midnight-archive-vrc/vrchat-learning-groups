Learning in VRChat

A directory of educational groups in VRChat: book clubs, discussion nights, talks and classes, language exchanges, sign language, trivia and world tours. Each VRChat group link opens the group's public page, no login needed. Member counts and schedules were taken from public group pages in November 2026 and may have changed since.

Where to find more events: VRChat events calendar (try the "education" and "exploration" categories) · VRC Search · VRCast events · VRMeet · Everything Immersive · r/VRchat

Listings come from public group pages and websites. Being listed doesn't mean a group is affiliated with or endorses this page. Compiled by Doghouse Devs, November 2026.
